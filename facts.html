DISTANCE

A light-year measures distance.

A light-year is the distance light travels in one year—not a measure of time. It is about 9.46 trillion kilometers.

NASA Science

OUR STAR

The Sun is a star.

The Sun is the star at the center of our solar system. Its energy comes from nuclear fusion in its hot core.

NASA Solar System Exploration

EARTH’S MOON

The Moon is tidally locked.

The Moon rotates once in about the same time it takes to orbit Earth, so we generally see the same lunar hemisphere.

NASA Science

MARS

Mars looks red because of rust.

Iron minerals in Martian rocks and dust oxidize, or rust, giving the planet its distinctive reddish appearance.

NASA Solar System Exploration

VENUS

A day on Venus is very long.

Venus rotates so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one trip around the Sun: about 243 Earth days.

NASA Solar System Exploration

JUPITER

Jupiter is the largest planet.

Jupiter is a gas giant and the biggest planet in our solar system. Its Great Red Spot is a long-lived storm.

NASA Solar System Exploration

SATURN

Saturn’s rings are mostly ice.

Saturn’s bright rings are made mainly of chunks of water ice, with smaller amounts of rocky material and dust.

NASA Solar System Exploration

BEYOND

Planets orbit other stars.

Exoplanets are planets beyond our solar system. Thousands have been confirmed, showing that planetary systems are common.
